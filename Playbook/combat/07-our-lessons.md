# Our lessons from fight builds v1 to v6 (7 to 8 Nov 2026)

What David caught that we should have caught first:

| Problem | Cause | Fix / rule |
|---|---|---|
| Backflip dodges | Used the free pack's acrobatic dodge clip | No flips. Slip/check/step-back (02-defence) |
| Punches not connecting | Strike played at the wrong range | Step to range first; check 4 |
| Striking from behind / not facing | No turn-to-face step before the strike | Face → step → plant → strike (check 1) |
| Both legs moving in a kick | Step-in overlapped the kick | Step finishes and plants before the kick starts (check 3) |
| Sliding walk, no real steps | Moving the root without stepping | Step-drag footwork matched to distance (check 6) |
| Top-heavy, unclear targets | Clips aim at one height; no load phase | Aimed variants (low kick to thigh, body dip) + visible load |
| Leg kick looked wrong | Free-pack kick leans back; foot contact | Shin on the thigh above the knee, pivot, no lean (01-strikes) |
| Fights too long / one-sided | Tuning | Check 12 every balance pass |
| Camera cropping / unreadable | Too close, swapping sides | 05-camera rules, check 10 |

Animation source limit: the free Martial Art Animations Sample has no slips, parries, hooks, side steps, directional hit reactions or a proper roundhouse. Some rules here can't be fully met until we have a proper animation source (Kubold Fighting Animset Pro ~$80 was the recommendation; David decides).
