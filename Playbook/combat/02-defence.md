# Defence: how it should look

David (8 Nov): "A dodge should just be moving out of the way... checking it." **No backflips.** UFC/boxing defence, small and efficient.

Coaching order of priority: **feet first, then head movement, then hands** (Evolve MMA). The guard is the backup, not the main tool. That gives a good skill gradient: novices block everything, experts slip and step.

| Defence | Looks right | Best against | Leaves open for a counter |
|---|---|---|---|
| High guard (our G) | Gloves/forearms pressed to the temples, elbows tucked to cover the ribs, chin down, a slight crouch. On impact, the guard is pushed back into the head and the feet slide a little | Hooks, head kicks | Body shots |
| Low guard / elbow block (our V) | Elbow drops to the hip, forearm covers the ribs/liver, slight turn toward the strike | Body punches, body kicks | Head |
| Slip | Small bend at the knees and a lean/rotate of the torso so the head moves just off the punch line (a few inches). Feet stay planted. Head returns to centre | Straight punches (jab, cross) | Return with one hand only |
| Roll (bob and weave) | U-shaped dip under the punch, bending the knees, not the waist | Hooks | Return with both hands |
| Parry | Palm (heel of the hand) taps the punch aside, small movement | Jabs | Immediate counter |
| Check (leg kicks) | Lead knee lifts and turns out so the kick hits the shin; toes pulled up | Low and body kicks | Counter cross |
| Step back / pull | One step back with the back foot first (step-drag backwards), then return | Anything; costs ground | Counter as they over-reach |
| Quick Step flank (our Q) | Lead foot steps out at an angle, pivot, now facing their side | Committed straight attacks | Flank strike ignores guard |

## Our game's Evade (E)
Should be a slip (vs punches), a check (vs low kicks) or a step back (vs body/head kicks), chosen automatically from the incoming strike. Never a jump or a flip.

## Common mistakes to watch for
- Dodging by stepping a full tile sideways when a slip is enough (looks like a dance).
- A guard with no impact reaction: blocks should push the blocker back a little, harder from stronger hits (Strength stat).
- Head movement at the waist with straight legs.
- Defence happening after the strike has already landed (timing: defence must START before the attacker's contact frame).
