# Fight camera

## Two cameras (Demo v2 step 4)
- **Tactical**: raised, angled view while picking moves and moving on the grid. Both fighters and the tiles clearly visible; tiles readable (David: side-on made tiles hard to see).
- **Exchange**: closer, cinematic, roughly side-on, while the 3 beats play out.

## The 180-degree line
- Imagine a line through both fighters. Keep the camera on ONE side of it during an exchange so left/right stay consistent (Hanson always screen-left, for example).
- If you must change sides, either cut through a shot directly on the line (neutral, from behind one fighter) or cross during big chaotic moments (a launch, a tumble). Break it on purpose only after it's established.
- Wide shots from both sides are fine if close-ups keep one screen direction (Kubrick note, Frame.io).

## Framing rules
- Both fighters fully in frame, feet included, during exchanges (feet show the footwork and the kicks).
- Leave space in the direction of a launch so the flight reads.
- Short push-in or slight zoom on heavy hits, then settle back.
- No cropping of heads or limbs at the contact frame.
- UI text (damage numbers, Vision reads) inside the safe area, not overlapping faces at contact.

## Mistakes from our builds
- Camera too close: limbs cut off, contact not visible.
- Camera swapping sides between beats: confusing who is who.
