# Characters (locked 26 Oct)

Set protagonist with a fixed name, face and backstory. The player steers his choices (like The Witcher). There's no character creator.

## Hanson (placeholder name, David still deciding)
- 16, the youngest child. Lean. From a normal, slightly poor family.
- Test result: plain **G rank, "low potential"**.
- Wants to be seen by his brother and sister. Believes they hate him and doesn't know why. Wants to prove himself but lacks what they have.
- No special reason for wanting to be a Ranker (like Ash wanting to be a Pokemon Master).
- Theme: finding and forging his own path.
- Look (concept brief): chestnut brown layered anime hair, green eyes, short neck, worn brown tunic with a patch, satchel strap across the chest, belt. Art: [assets/characters/hanson](../assets/characters/hanson/).

## Brother (half-brother)
- 4 years older. Same mum, different dad.
- Top student at Blue, being promoted to Gold for his final year with a big scholarship.
- Stoic and serious. Doesn't think much of Hanson.
- His real dad is successful and powerful, and looks down on him the same way he looks down on Hanson's family. The brother passes that coldness on to Hanson. Both sons are chasing approval.

## Sister
- 2 years older. At Green.
- Popular and mostly nice, but has a temper.
- Acts like she hates Hanson but loves him, and is the first to defend him.
