# The world (locked 26 Oct)

## Academy City
- The whole game takes place in Academy City, a massive city: guilds, dungeons, arenas, tournaments, rankings, politics, shops and a living society.
- **The Hero Guild** is the city's government.

## Ranks
- At 16 everyone takes an aptitude test, which gives a rank and a "scoped potential". Most people start at G.
- Ranks, low to high: G, F, E, D, C, B, A, S, SS, SSS.
- You get re-graded when you apply, or when you're flagged for doing far more than your grade suggests (an A who beats an SS).

## Rankers
- The top 100 in the city. Each is extremely powerful in their own way.
- You take a seat by challenging a Ranker and beating them.

## The four academies
| Academy | Who goes there |
|---|---|
| Brown | The poor |
| Green | Standard |
| Blue | Upper class |
| Gold | The elite |

- After the test there is one month (a breathing period) before enrolment. Whether you make a splash in that month decides your academy.
- A monthly tournament between the academies is a main attraction.

## Goals
- **Main quest:** become a Ranker. It's always there, with no ticking clock.
- **Your own path:** Skyrim-style freedom (a shop empire, training, underground street fighting and more).

## Vertical slice
